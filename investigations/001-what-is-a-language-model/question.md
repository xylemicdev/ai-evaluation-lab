# question

## investigation

what exactly is a language model?

### why this question?

language models sit at the center of modern ai systems, yet they are often described in ways that are either overly technical or misleading. people frequently talk about them as if they "think," "understand," or "know" things, while others reduce them to little more than autocomplete


before getting into how language models are evaluated, where they fail, or how they can be used effectively, i first need a clear mental model of what they actually are


this investigation is intended to build that foundation


## what am i trying to answer?

by the end of this investigation, i should be able to answer questions such as:

- what is a language model?
- what does it actually learn during training?
- why does it often appear to reason?
- what are the limits of that apparent reasoning?
- what are some common misconceptions about language models?


## what would success look like?

i should be able to explain what a language model is to someone with no ai background using clear, simple language without relying on buzzwords, vague analogies, or unnecessary technical jargon
