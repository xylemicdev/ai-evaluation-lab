# research

## research questions

1. how do researchers and ai practitioners currently classify language model failures?
2. what failure categories appear across different sources?
3. which of our seven categories have clear equivalents in existing research?
4. which of our categories don't map cleanly onto existing frameworks?
5. what important failure types did we miss?
6. where do different frameworks disagree about what should count as a distinct failure?
7. what does the way failures are classified tell us about how ai systems should be evaluated?
