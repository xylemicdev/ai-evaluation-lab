# principles

these priciples guide every experiment, document, and project in this lab

they exist to keep me honest

## understanding comes before tools

frameworks, models, and libraries will change

the ideas behind them matter more

before learning how to use something, it is important to understand why it exists

---

## observations come before conclusions

when running experiments, separate what happened from what i think happened

record the evidence first

interpret it second

---

## explain things simply

if an idea cannot be explained in clear language, i probably don't understand it well enough

simple explanations require deeper understanding than complicated ones

---

## every experiment answers a question

don't build things for the sake of building them

start with a question

design an experiment

record the outcome

decide what was learned

---

## failures are evidence

an experiment that disproves an assumption is still valuable

document what failed, why it failed, and what changed because of it


---

## prefer evidence over assumptions

whenever possible, test an idea instead of assuming it is true

curiosity should lead

assumptions should follow evidence

---

## document the reasoning

the conclusion matters

the path to the conclusion matters more

someone reading this lab should understand how each decision was reached

---

## build for clarity

good documentation is part of the engineering

someone should be able to understand a project without asking me to explain it

---

## improve continuously

this lab is not a collection of finished work

it is a record of continuous learning

changing my mind after discovering better evidence is progress, not inconsistency
