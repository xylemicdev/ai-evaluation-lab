# why

this repo exists because i want to understand ai systems well enough that i can trust my own judgement about them

i'm not here to collect buzzwords, chase trends, or build projects just because they are popular. i'm here to understand how language models work, where they succed, where they fail, and what it takes to build systems that people can actually rely on

this lab is my record of that journey

everything here is intentional. every note, experiment, benchmark, evaluation, and application exists because it helped answer a question or uncover something i didn't understand before

i care more about understanding than appearance

if i can't explain an idea simply, i don't understand it well enough

if an experiment fails, i'll document why

if i change my mind about something, i'll say so

if i make a mistake, i'll correct it instead of hiding it

the goal isn't to prove that i know everything

the goal is to become someone whose conclusions are earned through careful thinking, experimentation, and evidence


over time, i want this repo to grow into a public engineering lab. not a collection of disconnected projects, but a place where anyone can follow the process of learning how to evaluate, benchmark, and build reliable language model systems

if someone leaves this repo with one impression, i hope it's this:

this person doesn't just use ai

they understand why it behaves the way it does
